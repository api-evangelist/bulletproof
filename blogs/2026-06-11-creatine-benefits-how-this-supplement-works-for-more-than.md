For a long time, creatine has been closely linked with bodybuilders, strength athletes and serious gym-goers. And while it’s true that creatine can support muscle growth and exercise performance, that’s only one part of the story. Despite being one of the most widely researched dietary supplements available, creatine is still misunderstood.
