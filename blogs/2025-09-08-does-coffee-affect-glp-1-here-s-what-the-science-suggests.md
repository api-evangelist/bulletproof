Weight management drugs like Ozempic and Wegovy may be a hot topic, but they wouldn’t exist without the real deal: GLP-1. Short for glucagon-like peptide-1, GLP-1 is a hormone your body makes naturally. It plays a key role in blood sugar control, appetite regulation and satiety.
